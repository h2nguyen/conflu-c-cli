# ADR 0001 - Keep page content in files

## Status

Accepted for this example set.

## Decision

Keep selected Confluence pages in Markdown files and publish them with `conflu import-md-dir`.

## Consequences

- Reviews happen on files before publishing.
- `conflu export` can rebuild a reading copy when the source of truth changed in Confluence.
